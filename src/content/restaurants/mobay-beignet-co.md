Mo'Bay Beignet Co. is a new spot on Jeff Rd, and we're so happy to have a new place in Monrovia. We rarely get food options on this side of town.

We came for lunch, and there is a lot on the menu: burgers and chicken, and they also sell coffee and beignets. There is plenty of space to sit, an outdoor area, and they plan to have concerts in the fall. Plug-ins on the wall make it a good place to work, too.

We ordered burgers, the club chicken sandwich, onion rings, tater tots and beignets. Every item was fresh and well put together. The chicken sandwich was so crispy, and their sauce was one of our favorite things. The beignets were a hit. They are made fresh daily and come with syrups.

They also sell swag. If you've been, let us know what you think.
