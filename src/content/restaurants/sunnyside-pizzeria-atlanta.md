This one is a road-trip pick. If you ever make the drive to Atlanta, you have to try the Honey Hot Pizza at Sunnyside Pizzeria.

We got it with pepperoni, and it was fresh, hot, and cheesy, with the perfect amount of sweet heat. Another version at our table came with spinach, and that was really good too. We also tried the Alfredo pasta, which was pretty good, but the pizza was the win.

The drinks were good as well. We had a spritz that was very bubbly. The menu goes from wines to salads to pizzas to pastas, and they have gelato you can grab on the way out.

We sat on this one for a while, honestly. But it's too good to keep to ourselves, so we're done gatekeeping it.
