We stopped by Blue Oak BBQ in MidCity. It was Michaela's first time eating here, but we already love their margaritas, so of course we ordered a couple. On top of the regular menu, they have daily specials, and the place is really accommodating, especially for large groups.

The food came out pretty fast. Michaela got the Winter Burger, a Friday special, with fries. It was a very messy burger, but really good: spicy with jalapeno, crispy, and sweet too. Arrington got the mild buffalo wings with fries and baked beans, and the beans were really good.

We were basically fighting over the tater tots and fries. They were beer battered, hot and really, really good. We definitely want to go back.
