Everybody's out here looking for LA tacos in Alabama. We're just looking for good Mexican food, and Tacos de Canasta Acapulco is one of those spots we keep coming back to.

Our usual order is two chicken gorditas, a steak taco, a chicken taco, and nachos. Everything was delicious.

But those gorditas are the star. They're crispy on the outside and stuffed on the inside, and easily our favorite thing we ordered. If you go, get the gorditas.
