Everybody's out here looking for LA tacos in Alabama. You probably won't find California tacos here, but you will find some really good Mexican spots, and Tacos de Canasta Acapulco is one of those spots we keep coming back to. It's in the Harvest and Madison area, by the neighborhood Walmart.

Our usual order is two chicken gorditas, a steak taco, a chicken taco, and nachos. The tacos are good, and their tortillas are really nice.

But those gorditas are the star. This might be the best gordita we've had in Huntsville so far. The tortilla is crispy and well cooked, and the chicken inside is so flavorful that you don't really need the sauce, even though the sauce is good too.

One of us isn't a sour cream fan, so the nachos weren't a favorite, but they still got eaten, and that says something. If you go, get the gorditas.
