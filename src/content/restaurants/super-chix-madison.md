Super Chix did not disappoint. We hit the Madison spot by Toyota Field after a big day out, and let's just say a walk after the meal was necessary.

Here's what we ordered: a Buffalo Chicken Sandwich with regular fries, and five chicken tenders with the loaded fries. Yes, that's queso on top, and yes, it was worth it.

They have two locations, one in South Huntsville and one in Madison.
