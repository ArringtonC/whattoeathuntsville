Super Chix did not disappoint. We hit the Madison spot by Toyota Field after a big day out, and let's just say a walk after the meal was necessary.

We did not expect the portions to be this big. One of us got the Buffalo Chicken Sandwich, with pickles and sauce, plus a regular fry. That regular fry took up the whole plate. You could easily feed a family.

The five chicken tenders were nice and crispy, better than some of the tenders in Huntsville. They came with the loaded fries, topped with bacon and queso. Yes, it was worth it.

There are plenty of sauces: ranch, barbecue, Korean barbecue, their signature sauce, Nashville hot, buffalo, ketchup, and honey mustard. There's plenty of seating, including outside.

They have two locations, one in South Huntsville and one in Madison.
