If you don't know what to order at Steam Boys, start here. Get the popcorn chicken, the chicken cutlet gua bao, and the pan-seared beef bao.

For dumplings, go with the pan-seared chicken and the pan-seared cheeseburger dumplings. To drink, try the passion fruit boba tea or the mocha Oreo blended drink.

If you go on a Thursday, they have all-you-can-eat dumplings for $20.
