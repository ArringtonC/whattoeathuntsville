Steam Boys invited us in, and it's one of our favorite places. If you don't know what to order, start here.

The pan-seared beef bao is our go-to. The chicken cutlet gua bao was crispy and spicy. The popcorn chicken was really good, and one of us pretty much finished it alone.

For dumplings, go with the pan-seared chicken and the new pan-seared cheeseburger dumplings. The cheeseburger ones really do taste like a cheeseburger.

This was a first time trying boba for one of us. The passion fruit boba tea was a real surprise, and really good. For a sweet treat, one of us got the mocha Oreo blended drink.

If you go on a Thursday, they have all-you-can-eat dumplings for $20.
