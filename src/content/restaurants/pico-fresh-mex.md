Birria pizza, tacos, quesadillas, nachos, burritos. Pico Fresh Mex in Monrovia does not miss.

We come here almost every week, and the margarita pitchers might be part of the reason. They have deals and events throughout the week. We stayed quiet about it for a while because we do not want to wait in a line. The flavor is always there, the staff is really nice, and they work super fast. You can eat on the patio, at the bar or inside.

The birria pizza is our favorite. The fish taco is great, and you can customize it. Add the pickled onions. The nachos let you add up to four toppings, and we usually go with jalapenos, corn, lettuce and onion. The chicken tacos and the burrito were very flavorful, and the quesadilla was so good.

If you have not tried it yet, go. And go before the weekend.
