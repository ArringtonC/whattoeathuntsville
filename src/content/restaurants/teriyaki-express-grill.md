We tried Teriyaki Express Grill, a new food truck in Madison on the corner of Capshaw and Wall Triana. It parks in the gravel lot where they sometimes sell fireworks.

There are lots of options, with beef, chicken, and salmon teriyaki plates. It isn't badly priced either. We paid about $15 for the fried gyoza and a chicken teriyaki plate with rice and veggies.

It was a yum from us, very tasty and flavorful. It came with two sauces, the pink sauce you get at restaurants and a dipping sauce for the gyoza. The plate didn't even need the pink sauce, but it was a good addition.

Service was quicker than we expected. We've been back three or four times now, so it's worth a visit.
