We tried Teriyaki Express Grill, a new food truck in Madison on the corner of Capshaw and Wall Triana.

We got the fried gyoza and the chicken teriyaki with rice and veggies, and it was a yum from us. The plate didn't even need the pink sauce, but it was a good addition.
