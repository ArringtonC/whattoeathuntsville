We usually come to Metro Diner for brunch, but this time we stopped by for dinner. Good news: they serve breakfast for dinner too.

We got the loaded fries, fish and chips with cole slaw and tartar sauce, and steak tips with mashed potatoes and green beans, plus a little wine. Everything came out hot and crispy, and we kept the whole meal under $50.
