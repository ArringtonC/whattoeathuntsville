We usually come to Metro Diner for brunch, but this time we stopped by for dinner. Good news: they serve breakfast for dinner too.

We started with a little wine and the loaded fries, which came with crispy jalapenos and a big bowl of ranch. The portion was so big it could be a meal on its own.

The fish and chips seem to be a fan favorite in online reviews, and we can see why. The fish and fries were both crispy, the fish was still steaming, and the cole slaw was good and fresh. The steak tips came in gravy with mashed potatoes and green beans, and even those mashed potatoes surprised us.

Everything came out hot and crispy, and we kept the whole meal under $50. We were sleeping on their dinner menu. What's your go-to order here?
