Unreal. We give Hunnydew Bakery a 10 out of 10 across the board.

We tried a wide variety: cinnamon sparkle, chocolate lava, chocolate strawberry, and velvet dreams. Every single cookie was made fresh. The cinnamon sparkle felt like it had just come out of the oven, with no dryness at all. The strawberry one is part of her Valentine's Day collection, dipped and topped with strawberries, and we have never had a cookie like it. Velvet dreams mixes cookies and cream with red velvet, and it is the perfect combination.

We also warmed an old reliable chocolate chip cookie in the oven as a late-night sweet treat. So good.

The packaging is cute, and the ingredients are listed on the back, which helps if you have food sensitivities. Her cookies are gluten-friendly, and buying them helps support her medical treatments. This is a small local baker worth supporting.
