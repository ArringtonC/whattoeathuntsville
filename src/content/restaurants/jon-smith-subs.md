If we had to choose between Subway and Jon Smith Subs, we're picking Jon Smith every time.

We had been eyeing them for a while and finally stopped in for a lunch break. We did not know they specialize in hot sandwiches, and we love a good hot sandwich with peppers, onions, jalapeños, and cheese.

The hot Cajun chicken sandwich came out fresh and so hot we could not eat it all right away. It was so cheesy that we took the rest home. The fries on the side were just basic fries.

Tip: Monday through Friday they have a deal with a sandwich, chips, and a drink for $10, which is a pretty good deal. The staff were so nice. It's on County Line Rd, so it's an easy stop for a quick lunch, and it's now on our list of sandwich places.
