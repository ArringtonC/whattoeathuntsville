West End Grill is a Huntsville staple and a great cheap date. They've been around a while, and we admit we'd been sleeping on them. If you've lived here a while and still haven't been, we think it's worth a visit.

We got all of this for under $60: a Galaxy Lemonade with gin and curaçao, the loaded potato skins (a new item), a patty melt with tater tots, and two kinds of wings, Jamaican jerk and lemon pepper. Everything came out fresh and hot. That homemade ranch is one of the best we've ever had.

The lemon pepper rub is very lemony, and Arrington really liked it. The Jamaican rub was Michaela's favorite, with a sweet, tangy sauce. The patty melt had great reviews online and lived up to them: juicy burger, onions cooked just right.

Shoutout to Darrell for being a great server.
