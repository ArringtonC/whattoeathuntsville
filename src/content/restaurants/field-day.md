Field Day is the new spot Huntsville has been asking for: a place for adults to hang out where kids can come too. During the day it's for everyone, and at night it shifts to an adults-only party, which is really fun.

The games were the best part for us. We played putt putt, and they also have axe throwing, bowling and virtual games. We are very competitive people, so when we play, we play to win. There's a really nice outdoor area too, and it's a good place to watch NFL and SEC games.

On the food side, we sampled the appetizer platter with onion rings, chips and salsa, and mozzarella bites. The mozzarella bites had a little kick, which we liked. The pizza was amazing and probably our favorite thing on the menu, and we tried the wings too. To drink, they have fish bowls and plenty of drinks to share with friends.

We already have a favorite bartender. If you see him there, order a drink from him.
