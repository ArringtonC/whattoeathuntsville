Huntsville has a new Haitian food truck, and Chef Kay is on fire. We have already been back three times, which says enough.

We started with the vegetable stew, and the stew with that rice was amazing. The mac and cheese was so good, and they had the pikliz too. We also got the fried snapper, and when we say that fish was cooked to perfection, we mean it. It was our first time trying plantains, and they were pretty good.

On the next visit we got a little mix of everything, and the chicken and the pork were full of flavor. We also went back for that fish, because it was that good the first time. So far we have also tried the fried chicken stew and the jerk chicken. The jerk chicken is not on the menu, so ask if they have it.

Every plate comes with rice, mac and cheese, plantains and pikliz, so you get a full meal. If it is your first time trying Haitian food, start with the vegetable stew or the fried snapper fillet.

They open after 4pm. Five stars from us, and it is going on our list of hidden gems in Huntsville.
