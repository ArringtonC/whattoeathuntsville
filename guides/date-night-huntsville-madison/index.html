Date night restaurants in Huntsville and Madison

These are the date night spots we’ve actually tried in Huntsville and Madison. Some are dressy, and some are just a good meal for two. Here’s where we’d go back.

Chuck's FishSeafood · Clift Farm, Madison · $$$

A perfect date night at Clift Farm, with a nice vibe and nice service. It's a little pricey, but we think it's worth it.

Order: Hot honey pizza

Directions
Domaine SouthAmerican · Downtown, Huntsville · $$

Such a nice spot for date night downtown, with cocktails and great service. Reservations are suggested.

Order: Steak with duck fat fries

Directions
One ElevenAmerican · Downtown, Huntsville · $$$

Cute vibes, fun shareable drinks, and a photo booth for after dinner.

Order: Wedge salad

Directions
Cooper's HawkAmerican · Clift Farm, Madison · $$

Works for date night or any occasion, and the service stood out.

Order: S'mores budino

Hosted visit: the restaurant gave us our meal for free.

Directions
Osteria LucaItalian · Stovehouse, Huntsville · $$$

One of our new favorites, near Stovehouse. Great for date nights and worth every penny.

Order: Hot honey meatball pizza

Directions
Seoul GoodChicken · Stovehouse, Huntsville

Crunchy Korean fried chicken inside Stovehouse. Our order was the perfect amount of food for our date night.

Order: 16-piece combo: honey mustard and sweet & spicy

Directions
North ItaliaItalian · Bridge Street Town Centre, Huntsville · $$

A pretty space at Bridge Street that works for almost any occasion, with generous portions.

Order: Tripoline alfredo

Hosted visit: the restaurant gave us our meal for free.

Directions
