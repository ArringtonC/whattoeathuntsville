Best tacos in Huntsville and Madison

We are on a mission to try every taco spot around here. These are the ones we’d go back to, from food trucks to gas station counters. Got a favorite we missed? Tell us.

Taqueria Las AdelitasMexican · Madison Pike, Huntsville · $

One of the best tacos we've ever had. If you go for anything, get the fish taco.

Order: Fish taco

Directions
Taqueria Don PanchoMexican · Hwy 72 W, Madison · $

Some of the best birria we've had around here. The tortilla comes out crispy, not soggy, and it's not too oily.

Order: Birria tacos

Directions
Taqueria LunaMexican · Food truck, Madison · $

The birria tacos were the star of the show. Crispy, cheesy and everything.

Order: Birria tacos

Directions
Barracuda Taco StandMexican · Meridian St, Huntsville

Everything is made to order, and the tacos were the star. The pork taco with pickled onions was our favorite.

Order: Pork taco

Hosted visit: the restaurant gave us our meal for free.

Directions
Taqueria Doña ValeMexican · Wall Triana Hwy, Madison · $

Shredded chicken is a rare find here, and it was very flavorful. The tortillas are fresh and homemade.

Order: Shredded chicken tacos

Directions
Taqueria Los 3 Reales (food truck)Mexican · South Huntsville, Huntsville · $

Our South Huntsville taco truck. We're there pretty often. Bring cash.

Order: Steak tacos

Directions
Mi Tierra OaxaqueñaMexican · Pulaski Pike, Huntsville

We came for the tacos and they got a great rating from us. Corn or flour, both were good.

Order: Chicken and steak tacos

Directions
Pico Fresh MexMexican · Monrovia, Huntsville · $$

Our almost-every-week spot. The fish taco is great, and you should add the pickled onions.

Order: Fish taco

Directions
Los MorelensesMexican · Hwy 72 W / Old Railroad Bed, Madison · $

A gas station taqueria we went to almost every week for months. The go-to order includes chicken tacos on flour. Get the green and red sauce.

Order: Chicken tacos on flour

Directions
Mi Delicioso GuanajuatoMexican · Governors Dr, Huntsville · $

Everything came out hot and fresh. Muy delicioso.

Order: Quesabirrias with consomé

Directions
Tacos de Canasta AcapulcoMexican · Wall Triana Hwy, Madison · $

The tacos are good and the tortillas are really nice. Get a gordita too, it's the star.

Order: Steak taco

Directions
Tulum CantinaMexican · Martin Rd, Huntsville · $$

A rare sit-down spot for birria, and it comes with margaritas.

Order: Birria tacos

Directions
