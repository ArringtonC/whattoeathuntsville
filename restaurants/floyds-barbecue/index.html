Our take

Verified May 30, 2025

So many of you told us to try Floyd’s Barbecue, and now we get why. One of us has been coming here for about 20 years. We’re giving it 5 stars, and this is the best pork sandwich in Huntsville.

We went on a Friday. Online it may say they’re not open, but they are. We waited in line about 20 minutes.

We both got pork sandwich plates, one large and one small. The large comes with coleslaw on it, and even if you don’t like coleslaw, it works on this sandwich. One of us added a little of their sauce and liked it. For sides we picked fries, mac and cheese, and baked beans. The mac had a little pepper and was good, but the baked beans are the side you’ll really like. We also had to try a rib, and it was so juicy we ate it to the bone.

Start with the pork sandwich plate and you can’t go wrong.
