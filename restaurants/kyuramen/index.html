Our take

Verified May 22, 2026

We’ve been craving a good bowl of ramen bad lately, and Kyuramen delivered. We had seen some mixed reviews, so we wanted to test it for ourselves. We really enjoyed it. The staff were nice, the food was fresh, and the space looks great.

The Kyushu Spicy Tonkotsu Ramen at extra hot was the perfect spice level, and the pork melted in our mouths. The yakitori platter comes with shrimp, chicken, beef, pork belly, and zucchini, so it is great if you want a little of everything. The popcorn chicken did not look seasoned at first, but it was well seasoned, crispy, and fresh. The lemonade was super refreshing.

For dessert, the matcha tiramisu was a favorite, and the s’mores tiramisu was a 10 out of 10: chocolatey and melt-in-your-mouth.

Our order is honestly a solid place to start. Definitely give them a shot.

What we ordered

  • Kyushu Spicy Tonkotsu Ramen (extra hot)
  • Yakitori Platter
  • Popcorn Chicken
  • Matcha Tiramisu
  • S'mores Tiramisu
