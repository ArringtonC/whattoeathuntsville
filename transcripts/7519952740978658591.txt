I love me a low-key taco spot. This one is located in a gas station you guys in
Harvest and I've been eyeing them for a while so I decided to check them out.
They had very traditional Mexican food that kind of alluded to like street food
but they had a sitting area inside. We ordered four tacos, chicken steak and
also a chicken sope. I loved the chicken sope. It was a thick base to the
sope though. They had shredded chicken which is a rare find in Huntsville. It was
very flavorful. We also the steak tacos were really good too. Fresh-made tortillas
you can even tell looking at the quality of this that they these were homemade
tortillas which I love. They gave us green and red sauce to go on it but
overall really good experience. It kind of alluded to that authentic Mexican
flavor that you get when you go to Mexico so it was a nice throwback to our
trips. Let us know in the comments what you think.
