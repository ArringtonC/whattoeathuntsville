Okay, Iberacuda. Welcome to Huntsville. We were invited and of course we had to go for
research purposes. Here is our review. It is definitely giving laid-back vibes. A little
bit of California hint was in there, I don't know, but we ordered a mix of items, a chicken
taco, pork taco, crispy fish taco, and a couple of quesadillas. Starting with the tacos because
those were the star of the show. Everything is made to order and you can definitely tell
the pork taco was definitely our favorite. It was juicy, full of flavor, and that pickle
onion really set it off. The sauce was also a really good part of the meal. It made every
taco even better, but the chicken taco was a close second. The crispy chicken taco was
actually crispy and it was light. We also ordered a classic margarita. They have pictures,
which is really exciting. They were out of chips, so we didn't order any queso, so we
do have to go back and try that. Let us know in the comments if you've been in what you thought.
