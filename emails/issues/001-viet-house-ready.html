THIS WEEK'S PICK

Viet House

Vietnamese · Airport Rd, Huntsville · $$
If you've followed us for a while, you already know: Viet House is our forever favorite, and we end up here almost every week. Start with the fried spring rolls (the dumplings are great too), then get the chicken pho. Ask for level 1 if you want it a little spicy, or level 3 if you like it hot. We got all of it for under $40.
Order: Chicken pho, spicy · fried spring rolls · pork dumplings
