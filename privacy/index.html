Privacy

Last updated October 31, 2026.

What you send us

When you sign up for The Weekly Bite, we keep your email address so we can send the emails you asked for. We use Kit to store sign-ups and send our emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom.

When you suggest a restaurant or send a business inquiry, what you type in the form is emailed to us through FormSubmit so we can read it and reply.

We never sell your email address or anything else you send us.

Ads

We may show ads from Google. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ads Settings, and learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitor counting

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and our domain and email forwarding run through Cloudflare. Like any website host, they may keep basic logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site running and safe.

Contact

Questions or a request to delete your information: hello@whattoeathuntsville.com
